# Research

What was learned from outside: other tools, libraries, protocols and conventions, each claim marked with how it was
obtained. Research informs the decisions but decides nothing; each file's summary names the decisions it informed.

- [prior-art.md](prior-art.md): leaf, zk, basalt, obsidian.nvim, frogmouth, nb and glow; why none occupies slipbox's
  position; render-markdown.nvim's table allocator and its flaw.
- [stack.md](stack.md): Ink against OpenTUI, OpenTUI's `Markdown` against remark, and what installing OpenTUI,
  `mermaid-ascii` and the language-server libraries showed.
- [terminal-images.md](terminal-images.md): the three image protocols, why support can't be read from `$TERM`,
  multiplexers, and the details that bite.
- [link-conventions.md](link-conventions.md): how other tools give a link its meaning (roots, a leading `/`, names,
  moves), and how Obsidian and zk write one.
- [keymap-comparison.md](keymap-comparison.md): slipbox's keys beside vim's, VS Code's and Obsidian's; outdated in
  places.
