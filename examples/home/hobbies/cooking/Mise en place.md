## Reading the pantry

## Sending a recipe as text

What my friends see in a message is `[[Dal]]`, the brackets and all. A whole recipe card, pasted as it is:

```markdown
Before cooking read [[Dal]] and [[Shakshuka|the eggs]].
Photo: ![[dal.png]]
```

If I type it with a backslash, \[[Dal]] stays text in Obsidian too, and in a comment it is hidden:

<!-- Reminder: [[Dal]] needs more tamarind. -->

Outside code, [[Dal]] and [[Dal|the lentils]] are links in Obsidian and plain text to a command line.
