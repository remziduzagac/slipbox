# Notes

Notes on the recipes themselves: what to change next time.

- Dal: more tamarind, less chilli when Priya comes round.
- Shakshuka: the green version wants a lid for the last four minutes.
- Sourdough: the starter doubles in about five hours at 24 degrees, six in a cold kitchen.
- Flan: cool it overnight before turning it out. Patience, not technique.
