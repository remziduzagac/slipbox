# Red lentil dal

The dal I make when I get home late from a ride and want dinner in half an hour. Photo in [[Home]].

## Ingredients

- 250 g red lentils, rinsed until the water runs clear
- 1 onion, chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, sliced
- 1 thumb of ginger, grated
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 can chopped tomatoes
- 1 tsp tamarind paste
- 750 ml water and a good pinch of salt
- 2 tbsp ghee or oil
- 1 tsp cumin seeds, 1 dried chilli, a pinch of asafoetida
- Lemon and coriander to finish

Check the pantry first: [[pantry.csv]] says what is running low.

## Steps

1. Soften the onion in a little ghee, then add the garlic, ginger and turmeric for a minute.
2. Add the lentils, tomatoes, tamarind and water. Simmer for 20 minutes, stirring now and then, until it falls apart.
3. Make the tempering in a small pan: ghee, cumin seeds, chilli, asafoetida, ten seconds, no more. Pour it over the
   dal and put the lid on at once. ^tadka
4. Squeeze in the lemon, taste, salt, and scatter the coriander.
   - Leftovers thicken overnight; loosen with hot water. ^leftovers

> [!tip] The tempering
> It is the step that makes the dish. See [[Dal#^tadka]] above, and do not walk away from the pan.

## Variations

- With spinach stirred in at the end.
- With coconut milk instead of half the water.
- With a spoon of yoghurt, as Malik does.
