# Shakshuka

Eggs poached in a spiced tomato sauce, straight from the pan, with bread. One sauce, three ways.

## Classic version

Fry a sliced onion and a red pepper in olive oil until soft. Add 3 cloves of garlic, 1 tsp each of cumin, paprika and
a little cayenne. Pour in a can of tomatoes, simmer for 10 minutes, make four wells and crack an egg into each. Lid on,
six minutes. Parsley on top.

## Green version

Swap the tomatoes for a pile of spinach, chard and a handful of herbs, wilted with leeks and green chilli. Add a
splash of cream or a big spoon of yoghurt. Same wells, same eggs, but give it the lid for the last four minutes.

For the tomato-free days; the note on that is in [[Shakshuka#Green version]], which is also how it appears in the
meal plan on Thursday.

## With feta

Crumble feta over the classic version before the lid goes on. Skip the extra salt.
