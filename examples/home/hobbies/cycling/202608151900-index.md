# Cycling notebook

My riding, kept as a [zk](https://github.com/zk-org/zk) notebook: one note per ride, per piece of kit, per idea. Notes
are named by timestamp and slug, and links between them drop the `.md`, the way zk writes them.

## Rides

The big one this season was the [300 km brevet](rides/202609131900-300k-brevet) on 13 September: thirteen hours and
forty-one minutes, three controls, one wrong turn after the lower lake. The Kyoto trip gave me an easy
[evening loop along the Kamo](rides/202608301800-kyoto-river-loop), and I spent a Sunday on the
[gravel recon](rides/202609061000-gravel-recon) for next spring's route. Every ride since 2020 is a row in
[rides.csv](rides.csv); the [training log](202601010900-training-log-2026) has the weekly view.

## Routes

The brevet route is a single GeoJSON track, [300k-lakes-loop](routes/300k-lakes-loop.geojson), exported from Kestrel
and trimmed by hand: control points, the stops between them, the two detours, nothing else. I load the
[GPX copy](routes/300k-lakes-loop.gpx) onto the head unit the night before.
