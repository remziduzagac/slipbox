# Bike fit

Fit session on 20 August with the shop's fitter, the day before I flew to Kyoto. The numbers below are what we ended
on; I measured the old setup the night before so I could see what moved. Back to the [index](/202608151900-index).

| Measure | Before | After | Change |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Saddle height | 728 mm | 724 mm | −4 mm |
| Saddle setback | 62 mm | 68 mm | +6 mm |
| Reach (saddle to bar) | 548 mm | 540 mm | −8 mm |
| Stack drop | 55 mm | 45 mm | −10 mm |
| Stem length | 100 mm | 90 mm | −10 mm |
| Crank length | 172.5 mm | 170 mm | −2.5 mm |
| Cleat position | 6 mm back | 9 mm back | +3 mm |
| Bar width | 420 mm | 420 mm | 0 mm |

## Why

My knees complained on the long rides in June. The fitter measured a bit too much extension at the bottom of the
stroke and not enough weight on my hands; moving the saddle back and down, and shortening the stem, evened it out.
The shorter cranks were the surprise: pedalling feels quicker and my hips stay quiet.

## Check after the brevet

- Numbness in the left hand after hour ten? Only for a few minutes at stop 7, the third control.
- Knees: no complaints at all.
- Lower back: tight in the last two hours; try 5 mm less drop at the next adjustment.
