# Lights

Comparison before the brevet, when I had to ride a night section with no street lighting. Lumens are the maker's
number on the highest steady mode; runtime is what I measured on the lowest mode I would actually ride on.

| Light | Mount | Lumens | Runtime (h) | Weight (g) | Charge |
| :--- | :---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | :---: |
| Ridgeline 1200 | bar | 1200 | 6.5 | 168 | USB-C |
| Ridgeline 600 | helmet | 600 | 9.0 | 98 | USB-C |
| Lantern Mini | bar | 450 | 11.5 | 74 | micro-USB |
| Tail Blinker | seatpost | 80 | 24.0 | 36 | USB-C |
| Tail Blinker (flash) | seatpost | 80 | 60.0 | 36 | USB-C |

| Mode | Lumens | Beam | Use for |
| --- | :---: | ---: | :--- |
| Steady high | 1200 | 70 m | unlit descents |
| Steady mid | 600 | 45 m | unlit flat roads |
| Steady low | 200 | 25 m | towns |
| Flash | 80 | – | daylight, rear only |

Two bar lights plus the helmet light give about 27 hours between them if I swap on the fly, which is enough for the
300 and, with a charge at a control, for a 400.
