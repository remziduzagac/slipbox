# Winter layers

What I wear below 5 °C, worked out over two winters of commuting and the odd cold Sunday. Linked now from the
[index](../202608151900-index) of the notebook, and it keeps growing as I find what works. The same list is linked from
the [index](/202608151900-index) under gear.

## Below 5 °C

- Merino base layer, long sleeve, tucked in
- Thermal bib tights, with the pad
- Windproof jersey over a light fleece, zip half open on climbs
- Overshoes (neoprene), not covers; covers are only for rain
- Thin wool cap under the helmet, neck tube up over the chin
- Lobster gloves, with liners if it is below 0 °C

## Below 0 °C

Everything above, plus a second pair of socks one size up, chemical toe warmers on the long rides, and a thermos at
the first stop. Beyond −5 °C I take the fixed bike, because the gears freeze first.

## Lights in winter

Dark at half past four from November. See the [lights comparison](../gear/202609011200-lights.md) for what I run; the
short version is two on the bars and one on the helmet, with the flashing rear at all times.

## Mistakes I will not repeat

- Cotton anything.
- Waterproof jacket with no vents: soaked from the inside in twenty minutes.
- Gloves that fit exactly; cold hands swell and the fingers go numb.
