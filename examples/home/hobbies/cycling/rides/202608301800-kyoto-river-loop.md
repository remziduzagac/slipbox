# Kyoto river loop

The last evening of the trip, 30 August. Rented a city bike near 四条大橋 (Shijō Ōhashi) and rode up the east bank of
the Kamo river to 北山大橋 (Kitayama Ōhashi), came back down the west bank by way of the stepping stones at 出町柳
(Demachiyanagi), and carried on south along the river path to the Katsura confluence and back up in the dark. 42.6 km
with a long coffee stop and a ramen detour; very flat, very warm. Photos and the rest of the trip are in the
[Kyoto notes](slipbox://photography/trips/京都/index.md).

## What I noticed

Locals run the path in both directions; there is a quiet convention of keeping left, with a bell only for the
tourists. The river is shallow enough for herons. 夕焼け, the sunset glow, came at 18:40 and lit the whole east bank
orange for ten minutes; I was stopped for it by the third bridge and the camera stayed in the bag, which is a first.

## Numbers

- Distance 42.6 km, moving time 2 h 08, average 20 km/h
- Climb 190 m, most of it the bridges
- Temperature 31 °C at six, 26 °C at nine
- Food: おにぎり, a melon pan, iced barley tea 麦茶 twice

Back home I would like to ride this again with Aiko, who grew up near the 鴨川 and says the best bakery is by 荒神橋.
