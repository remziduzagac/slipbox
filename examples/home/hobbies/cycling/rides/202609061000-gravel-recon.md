# Gravel recon

Sunday 6 September, 63.8 km on the gravel bike. The idea is a 200 km gravel route for next spring that starts and ends
at the station, links the forest tracks, and avoids the main road after the reservoir. I rode the western half to see
what is really rideable; the [bike fit](../gear/202608201000-bike-fti) changes from August made it
comfortable for the whole morning, which I had not expected on gravel.

## Findings

- The track north of the reservoir is wider than it looks on the map and fast when dry.
- Two locked gates between the reservoir and the first village; either carry over or go round by the farm.
- The ford at km 41 is knee deep after rain; fine in August, not in March.
- Good water tap at the cemetery in the village.

## The route so far

A rough sketch is in my head and not yet in a note: [a draft of the route](../ideas/gravel-200-spring) should come
from Kestrel once I have clean traces for the second half. Until then the nearest thing is the brevet track, which
shares the first twelve kilometres. There is a post on [[gravel-gps-tips]] I want to find again about trimming a trace
to the gate.
