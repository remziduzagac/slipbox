# 300k brevet

Sunday 13 September. 301.4 km, 2 860 m of climbing, 13 h 41 min elapsed from the 06:00 start to the final stamp, well
inside the time limit. My longest day on a bike. The track I rode is
[the route file](../routes/300k-lakes-loop.geojson), and the [index](/202608151900-index) has the rest of the context.

Clock times are from my head unit; the brevet card has the same stamps to the minute, give or take the volunteers'
watches. Stopped time came to 98 minutes, so about twelve hours of it was riding.

## Stop 1: Mill Lane

Quiet roads, mist until the first climb. Banana and a coffee.

## Weather

Four degrees at the start, ground fog in the low fields, clearing by seven. Light north wind.

## Stop 2: Aldworth bakery

Control one: a queue out of the door and a woman who stamped the card without looking up. Filled the bottles at the
churchyard tap, where a man gave me a flapjack and asked where I was heading; I said "home".

## Weather

Sun on the green, 11 °C, still. The best conditions of the day; I shed the arm warmers.

## Stop 3: Market square

Soup and a sandwich. Changed socks. The legs began to feel the 124 km.

## Weather

Warm and humid, 19 °C, a few spots of rain on the way in that came to nothing.

## Stop 4: Hollin Cross

Petrol station again; a very bad coffee but a decent flapjack. Put on the helmet light as a precaution.

## Weather

Headwind, 17 °C and steady rain for the hour before and after. Soaked through by the next climb.

## Stop 5: Lower lake

Control two, and the kerb of the car park: rain stopped as I arrived, so I wrung out the gloves and ate a sandwich and
a tin of rice pudding sitting on the kerb, and nearly did not stand up again. Took the signed detour round the flooded
ford after it, and the wrong turn I made beyond it cost me three kilometres.

## Weather

Clearing sky behind a cold front, 13 °C, a strong gust from the north on the dam road, then calm.

## Stop 6: Village hall

Volunteers with tea and flapjacks, a clock on the wall that agreed with nobody.
Fitted the bar light for the last stretch.

## Weather

Mid-afternoon, 16 °C, long shadows across the fields, a headwind that arrived at exactly the wrong moment and then
dropped.

## Stop 7: Bridge services

Control three. Fluorescent lighting, crisps, a coffee that tasted like pennies. Only 63 km to go and I knew I would
finish.

## Weather

Cooling fast, 12 °C, low sun in my eyes, mist starting in the river hollows, no wind at all.

## Stop 8: Upper lake

Boat house shuttered, but the bench had a view and a tailwind to the hill beyond. Two other riders pulled in; we agreed
the clock no longer mattered and rolled out together.

## Weather

Low sun on the water, 12 °C, still, the arm warmers back on.

## Stop 9: Finish

Lakeside car park, the organiser with the stamp. Hot chocolate. Phoned home from a bench.

## Weather

Nine degrees, dusk, dew on the saddle and a thin moon already up over the car park.

## Stop 10: Home

The ride home from the car park took twelve minutes, lights on; I fell asleep with my shoes on.

## Weather

Light drizzle at the door, 8 °C and dark. Just enough to remember I had not washed the bike.

## What I would change

- Eat earlier: I was behind on calories at the market square and paid for it on the next climb.
- Bring a real rain jacket, not the packable one.
- Use the dynamo question from the [index](/202608151900-index) as a reason to buy a hub.
