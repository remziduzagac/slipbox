# Camera bodies

What I own, what I have rented, and what I would rent again. The first table is the spec sheet I keep for every body
at the same twenty columns, so two bodies compare by eye; the second is the lens kit for the Fuji bodies, shorter on
purpose. Prices are what I paid or would pay new, in euros. `Yes*` means weather resistant with the lens it came with.

Notes: the X-T5 is the body I took to Kyoto in August, with the 16-55, the 35 and the 56. The GR IIIx lived in a
jacket pocket there, and was the only camera on the night walk around Pontocho and on the Gion day. I rented the Z f
for a weekend in June and gave it back; the dials are lovely, the file size is a step down from what I am used to. The
Q3 is a wish, not a plan.
