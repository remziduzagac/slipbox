# Lens comparison

The lenses I have owned or borrowed, one row each. The codes are the catalogue and firmware strings from my insurance
inventory, so I can look a lens up by what the paperwork calls it; what Lightroom itself writes into the files is the
shorter `LensModel` in [the August export](../exports/exif-2026-08.csv). The notes are what I remember and what I wrote
down in the field. For the bodies they sit on, see [bodies](bodies.md).

A 26.1 mm lens, about 40 mm in full-frame terms, at f/2.8 sounds ordinary on a spec sheet, but the pairing with the sensor is exceptional: corner to corner it resolves more than the files can hold, distortion is corrected so cleanly that I never think about it, and the macro mode focuses to 12 cm, close enough to photograph a coin on a table. It has no filter thread without the adapter ring, which I bought, no hood unless I fit the adapter, which I did not, and a lens cap that falls off in the pocket, which I solved with tape. What it gives back is a camera that disappears. On the night walk through Pontocho on 25 August I took 211 frames in ninety minutes without once raising the camera to my eye; I shot from the hip at 1/125, f/5.6 and ISO 1600, snap-focused at two metres, and I kept 9. The hit rate is the best I have had with any lens, and none of the keepers is a frame I could have composed through a viewfinder. The one real problem is dust: a speck on the sensor appeared in June and sits in the upper left corner of every frame at f/8 or smaller. I have cleaned it twice with a blower and the speck is still there. I will send the body to the service centre in November. |

## Which one I carry

For a day of walking, the 35 f/1.4 and the GR, nothing else. For a trip, the 16-55 on the X-T5 and the GR in a pocket.
The 56 comes out for portraits and for the harbour walks, where I stand still and wait for the light.

The Kyoto day lists, with the lens used for each frame, are in [the trip folder](../trips/京都/index.md). The same
table is linked from my notes as `slipbox://photography/gear/lenses.md`, which is how the index of my notes points
here without a relative path.
