# 伏見稲荷 Fushimi Inari, 24 August

Up at 04:40 to be at the lower gate before the first buses. The mountain path takes two hours at a photographer's pace,
and the whole of it is covered by gates, so the light comes in as stripes.

![朝の千本鳥居](../../images/inari-01.jpg)

## 千本鳥居の朝

千本鳥居は、本当は千本もない。数えたら八百と少しだったと、愛子さんが後で教えてくれた。それでも、朝五時五十分、誰もい
ない参道に朱色が続くのを前にすると、数は問題ではなくなる。「稲荷山」の頂上までは登らず、四ツ辻で引き返した。

## 夕方の二回目

Back at 17:30, with the zoom racked out to 55 and the tripod, for the same gates in the other light. The light was
copper, the crowds thicker, and I kept only four frames. Of the first visit I kept forty.
