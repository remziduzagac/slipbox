# Wednesday 19 August

Interview day. [Jo](../people/jo-becker.md) lined up P02, a weekend roadie in Leeds, for half an hour, and I sat in
with the camera off and a notebook open, then listened again to the recording of P01's interview from yesterday. The
write-up lives in [user interviews, August 2026](../research/user-interviews-2026-08.md); this is only the diary of the day.

## Between calls

The  first  rider  planned  every  commute  the  night  before.  The   second   never   planned   anything   and
opened the app only when she was already lost; she said,	the tab key is how I take notes on a train, that
she trusted the app until the day it sent her over a closed bridge.

Nobody mentioned sharing a route on their own. When I asked, both said they would send a screenshot to a friend,
which is the answer that makes Tomás happy and me nervous.
