## The merge

Priya merged `feature/route-sharing` into main at 17:40, with a merge commit and not a squash, because she wants the
history of the branch to stay readable. The merge brought in three commits from two people, two of mine and one of
Tomás's, which is the first time his code is in the git log; until now it was only his sharing doc.

After the merge the file explorer showed the new and changed files with their marks: the docs for route sharing and the
new files under `src/routes/` show up as changed. I like that view for a merge; it is the quickest way to see what
actually landed instead of what the PR description said landed.
