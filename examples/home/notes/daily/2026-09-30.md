## Jo's first replies

[Jo](../people/jo-becker.md) sent the first batch at lunchtime and I read them standing up, which is the only way I can
read feedback.

- "Sent a route to my brother and he opened it on the web without installing anything. He bought the app an hour later."
- "The offline thing finally works for the Alps. Thank you."
- "경로 공유 기능 정말 좋아요! 친구들과 주말 라이딩 코스를 바로 나눌 수 있어서 편해요."
- "Why does the share button say Share and not Send? Asking for my mother."
- "Everything is fine, which is the strangest thing I have ever written to a software company."

The Korean one is from a rider in Seoul, who found out about the app from a cycling forum. I do not read Korean, but
I can tell from the exclamation mark that it is a happy one; Jo translated the rest: the sharing is the part they like.
