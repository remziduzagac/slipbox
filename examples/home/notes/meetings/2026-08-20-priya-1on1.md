# 1:1 with Priya, 20 August

Thursday, 30 minutes, before I leave for Kyoto tomorrow evening. [Priya](../people/priya-nair.md) had the agenda open

## Q3 wrap

Route sharing is in review and the offline sync rewrite is two weeks behind my first estimate. We agreed the slip is

30 September holds if the queue work lands by the middle of September.

- Shipped in Q3: tile cache v2, the elevation fix, the new onboarding

## Team load

Aiko is carrying the sync rewrite almost alone, and Malik is the only person who can read the queue metrics. Priya
asked whether that is a risk or just a fact.

> I would call it a risk the day one of them takes a holiday and the other one cannot answer a page. Today it is
> only a fact. Fix it before September, while nobody is on call alone.
>
> And please stop answering the queue questions yourself in the channel. People learn who to ask by watching who
> answers.

That was her feedback on how I handle load, and it is fair. I have been the fastest answerer for a year, which is
the opposite of a staff engineer's job.
