# Dr. Lena Okafor

Outreach coordinator at the Halden Ridge Observatory, where I volunteer on outreach nights. Astronomer by training,
teacher by habit. She runs the Friday nights and decides who works the dome and who works the car park.

## Working together

- Sends the rota on the first of the month; replies fast if you answer fast
- Always asks if I have eaten before she asks about the telescope
- Does not want any app at the dome: paper signup sheet only
- Likes to hear "no" the day before, not at the door

## 1:1s and reviews

Not a manager, so there are no 1:1s, only the nights themselves. Planning notes are in the
[outreach project](../projects/halden-outreach.md), and the volunteer log lives with the dome-night note, see
[dome night volunteering](../projects/dome-night-volunteering.md).

At the observatory this autumn she has [Kenji Mori](../../../vault/people/kenji-mori.md), a visiting observer who is
on Aster two nights a week and sometimes stays for the public night. That note is in the observatory's own vault,
beside this folder. Back to the [index](../index.md).

## Birthday

9 September. The staff bring a cake to the Friday night nearest it.
