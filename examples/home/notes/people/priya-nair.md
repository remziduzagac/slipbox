# Priya Nair

Engineering manager at Kestrel Labs, my manager since 2024. She runs the team's planning, hiring and on-call rota, and
reads everything I send her twice. The people who say "ask Priya" are right.

## Working together

- Likes a written agenda the day before, and short bullet notes after
- Says the hard thing first and expects the same; never softens a "no" into a "maybe"
- Reads on paper: send her the PDF if it is more than two pages
- Best slot: Thursday after lunch. Worst: Monday before ten
- Gets the *why* of a decision before the *what*

## Agenda

Her standing 1:1 agenda, which I copy to the top of each meeting note:

1. Anything blocking, from either side
2. Team load and on-call: who is tired
3. Decisions I made that she should hear from me
4. Growth: promo packet, mentoring, the next year
5. Anything not on this list
