# Tomás Ruiz

Product designer at Kestrel Labs, and the reason the route page does not look like a spreadsheet. He sketches on paper
first, always, then redraws it in Figma so that I can pretend it was digital.

## Working together

- Show him the problem, not the solution; he will tell you if the solution is wrong
- Wants real data in every mock: real route names, real distances, real accented characters
- Asks "what happens when it is empty?" for every screen, and is right every time
- His name is spelled with an accent. Tomás and Tomas are the same person in my notes, and search treats them alike

## Where he shows up

- Route sharing: the share sheet, the three states, the revoke button nobody has found a place for
- Route sharing's reach: KR1.2, non-member link opens, his Q4 key result (club onboarding was picked at the planning
  meeting and dropped from the final)
- PRDs, as the second author, and design reviews
