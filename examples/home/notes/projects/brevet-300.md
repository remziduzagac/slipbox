# Brevet 300

The 300 km brevet on 2026-09-13, planned here and ridden. The ride report is in the cycling notebook:
[the 300k brevet](../../hobbies/cycling/rides/202609131900-300k-brevet.md). It is a zk notebook with no name, so I link

an unnamed root cannot be pointed at: [bike fit](slipbox://cycling/gear/bike-fit.md).

Tags this plan carries: #project/brevet and #health/cycling. It was a #project all summer.

## Training

Twelve weeks, three rides a week, one long. The long ride peaked at 237 km on 26 July. Kyoto then cost me nine days off
the bike and I rode the river loop to get back into it; the first week of September was a 121 km Lakes loop over the
brevet's first 40 km, plus a lights test. The plan survived it.

## Food

Eat before hungry, drink before thirsty. One bottle an hour, 60 g of carbohydrate an hour, real food after 150 km.
The official controls are at 86 km, 171 km and 238 km: pastry at the bakery, the big meal at the car park, whatever
stays down at the services. In between, soup at the café at 124 km and whatever the garage sells at 150 km.

## Things I learned

The route was one loop around the lakes; the track is `routes/300k-lakes-loop.geojson` in the notebook (the GPX is its
copy, `routes/300k-lakes-loop.gpx`), and I should have checked the second control's opening hours the day before,
not at the control.
