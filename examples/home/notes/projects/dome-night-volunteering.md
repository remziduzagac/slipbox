# Dome night volunteering

I started this from the observatory's TOML-frontmatter template, so the properties above are TOML, not YAML. The
general plan is in [halden-outreach](halden-outreach.md); this note is only about the nights I have signed up for.

## The nights

| Night | Role | Telescope | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2026-10-16 | guide at the eyepiece | Aster | Saturn is low but clear |
| 2026-10-30 | door and queue | none | school group, 40 children |
| 2026-11-13 | guide at the eyepiece | Brill | the Andromeda galaxy before the moon rises |

## Before each night

- Read the evening's observing list and learn three things worth saying about each object.
- Bring red torches; nobody uses a white one under the dome.
- Tell Priya I leave at 17:30 on those Fridays; she already agreed.

## The refit

The shutter that jams was due to be replaced over the summer shutdown, and the work slipped. The plan for it is in the
observatory's own notes: [dome refit](../../../vault/projects/dome-refit.md). If it is not done by the 30th, the
school night moves to the lecture room and there is no dome at all.
