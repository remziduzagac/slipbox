# Home office

The spare room becomes an office before the winter. I measured twice; the numbers use no-break spaces so a unit never
lands alone at the end of a line.

## Measurements

The alcove is 312 cm wide and 240 cm deep, with a ceiling at 2.45 m. The desk is 160 cm by 80 cm, which leaves
76 cm to the wall on the window side and 152 cm on the door side. The window sill is at 90 cm, so the monitor arm
has to clamp to the desk, not the wall. A standing desk raised to 118 cm puts the screen top at 168 cm, which is
about my eye line plus the stand.
