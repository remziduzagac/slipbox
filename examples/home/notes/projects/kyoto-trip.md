# Kyoto trip

Nine days, 22 to 30 August, with two cameras (the X-T5 and the GR) and no laptop. Aiko wrote the Japanese captions for the photographs and
corrected my place names; her versions are in the tables. The full itinerary lives with the photographs, in a vault
named `photography`: [the itinerary](slipbox://photography/trips/京都/itinerary.md).

The photo notes sit in a folder named after the city, so the path has Japanese in it. Here is the same index, three
ways, depending on where I am writing from:

- raw: [trip index](../../hobbies/photography/trips/京都/index.md)
- percent-encoded: [trip index, encoded](../../hobbies/photography/trips/%E4%BA%AC%E9%83%BD/index.md)
- by pointer, the best of the three: [trip index, by pointer](slipbox://photography/trips/京都/index.md)

## Practical

Flew on the 21st and arrived on the 22nd. A rail pass for the week, a rented bicycle for two days (the river loop is in
the cycling notebook, [the Kyoto river loop](../../hobbies/cycling/rides/202608301800-kyoto-river-loop.md)), and cash
for the temples. Aiko's rule for the trip: one good photograph a day is a success.

## After

I kept 214 frames, cut to 40 for the selects and to 10 for print. The trip also showed up in my [reading list](../reading/list.md)
through the essays I carried.
