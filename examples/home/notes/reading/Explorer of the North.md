# Explorer of the North

A biography of a polar surveyor, read on the train in September. Its maps are why I picked it up: they were drawn by
walking, a day's pace at a time, the way a brevet's route sheet is.

Outline of the book, by part:

1. The early surveys, on foot and by sledge.
2. The winter at the station, and the maps made there.
3. The return, and what the maps were used for.

## Quotes

> The map is finished when the walking is.
