# Reading list

What I am reading, what I have read, and what I keep meaning to. The notes I wrote on a book are linked from its row.
The list is also where I practise links, so it uses more forms than it needs to.

## Reading now

- [The Quiet Queue](the-quiet-queue.md "notes on the book") -- backpressure, for engineers; my notes cite pages.
- [Maps and Territories](maps-and-territories.md 'my notes') -- a cartographer's history of projections, slowly.
- [The Long Ride](the-long-ride.md (a memoir)) -- a randonneur's year; I read it in the saddle's shadow, a chapter a
  night.
