## How we recruited

Through the app's own settings page, one banner for two weeks, and a plain question: would you talk to us for half an
hour about how you plan a ride? Forty-one said yes; Jo and I picked ten for a spread of rider types. Everyone got a
voucher. Nobody was asked about anything they could not decide to tell us.

## Handling

- Codes only in notes, summaries and slides.
- Recordings are deleted after the transcript is checked, which is the week after.
- A participant who withdraws is removed from every note on the same day, here included.

The raw notes are in [user interviews](user-interviews-2026-08.md).
