# User interviews, August 2026

Raw notes from ten half-hour interviews, 18 August to 2 September, before the synthesis. Participants are codes;
[the list](contacts.md) says who is who by rider type. Quotes are as spoken, translated where they were not in English.
The synthesis is Jo's, in the product repository; this is what I wrote while listening. Tags on the note:
#research/interview and #research/sharing.

## What people do now

Most riders plan on a laptop and ride from a phone, and they trust neither to sync. Four of ten carry a paper copy of
the route on long days.

> "I plan at the kitchen table and I never know if it got to my phone, so I screenshot it."
> -- P01, commuter

> "If it says offline, I believe it. If it says nothing, I do not."
>
> The second sentence came after a pause, and she laughed.
> -- P06, new rider

## Sharing

Sharing is mostly one rider sending a route to another, over a chat app, with a sentence. They want the recipient to see
the turns and not to need an account. Three said they would pay for a group route.

> A shared link that asks me to sign up has failed.
> -- P05, brevet rider

Questions to follow up on:

1. Do they share routes they have not ridden?
2. How long should a link live?
3. What do they do with a link someone sent them a year ago?

## Offline

P03 plans rides around a hobby: a small telescope in the pannier on gravel nights, so every route ends at a dark
field. Offline matters most there, because there is no signal at all. Aiko laughed when I told her.

> Retry? I do not retry. I stand there holding the phone up like a sacrifice.
> -- P03, gravel rider

## Group rides

P09 leads a Sunday club ride and was the most specific about what a shared route has to do.

> I send the route on Thursday and by Saturday night four people have asked me which one it was, because they have
> three versions in three chats.
>
> What I want is one link that I can change, and that everyone sees change. Not a new link every time I fix a turn.

Two of the others in the group had told me the same thing before, which is why I asked her;
[the synthesis](slipbox://product/research/synthesis-2026-09.md) later found it too.

> The club leader told me something I think about on every ride:
>
> > A route is a promise to the slowest rider. If the promise changes on the road, you have broken it.
>
> and then he laughed and said he was not that wise, he just hated waiting at junctions. -- P09, reporting
> her club's leader

## What I want from the next round

- Ask the same question a different way and see if the answer changes.
- Watch someone plan a route instead of asking about it.
- Talk to two people who have stopped using the app.
