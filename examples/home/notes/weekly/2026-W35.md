# Week 35, 24 to 30 August

Kyoto week. I was away from the keyboard from Monday to Sunday, so this review is mostly a list of photographs and the
two or three things I did not leave undone. Everything about the trip is in [Kyoto trip](../projects/kyoto-trip.md),
and the photographs have their own place: [the trip folder](../../hobbies/photography/trips/京都/index.md).

## What happened

- Fushimi Inari at dawn on Monday, the best morning of the trip
- The camera shop in Teramachi, where I bought nothing
- Aiko's restaurant, twice
- Tō-ji at opening time on Tuesday, and the river by bike on Sunday, last thing before the flight

Nine days of pictures is a lot to look at, so I have been selecting in the evenings: 214 frames kept from about
2 100, and forty of those are the selects.

## Quote of the week

The camera shop owner, through Aiko's translation of the message he sent afterwards:

> The shutter is not a button you press. It is a door you open for one fortieth of a second, and the light that comes
> in is the only thing that was ever yours to give.
>
> Come back when you have used a whole roll.

>
> He signed it with his shop's name and a small drawing of a fox.

## Something Malik taught me

At lunch before I left, Malik taught me what you say to somebody who has just finished work: يعطيك العافية, roughly "may
you keep your health", and the answer is الله يعافيك. I said it to the hotel receptionist in Kyoto, who looked at me as
if I had pulled a rabbit out of a hat, and then said something very kind in Japanese that I did not catch.

I wrote both on the back of the receipt for Malik to check, in his script and not my transliteration, and he corrected
only the second one.
