# Week 39, 21 to 27 September

The incident week. The week of the 14th has its own short review in [week 38](2026-W38.md). This one starts on Monday
the 21st, which is a day I left blank on purpose, and is mostly the sync queue. #review/weekly

## The numbers

- Stall: 2 h 32 min from the first alert at 13:52 to a queue back under 50 at 16:24; customers felt it from 14:05
- Messages stuck: **one**, and **a *really* poisonous one at that**
- Queue depth at the peak: 5 962
- Customers who wrote in: 3 on the day, 11 by Friday
- Hours I spent on it: 14, of which 6 were reading code I wrote in March

## Decisions

The review on Thursday, [the sync queue incident review](../meetings/2026-09-24-sync-queue-incident-review.md), made the
decisions. These are the ones that survived the meeting; the struck-out ones did not.

- Cap the retry delay at sixty seconds
- Alert on the age of the oldest message
- ~~Restart the workers every night~~ (Malik, correctly: "a coin flip on a schedule")
- ~~Add a second queue for poison messages~~ (Priya: later, if the cap is not enough)
- ~~Write the runbook after the release~~ it goes before the release, in 2.3
- Move the runbook into `runbooks/` and add a section on skipping a message[^1]

## What I learned

Three things, in order of how much they hurt:

1. ***Read the retry code.*** I trusted it in March and I would have found the missing cap in five minutes.
2. __Alert on age, not depth.__ Depth is a guess about the future; age is a fact about the past.
3. *Write the runbook for the thing that happened, not the thing you hoped would.*

~~Never again~~ is not a plan either. The plan is the ceiling, the alert and the runbook.
