# Week 40, 28 September to 4 October

what happened after. #review/weekly #release #project/kestrel

The plan I worked from is in the product repository: [the 2.3 plan](slipbox://product/releases/2.3-plan.md) as a pointer

## What I want to remember about the numbers

Route sharing links were opened 1 140 times on the first day, by 612 people who did not have the app, and 31 of them
installed it. That is the number the Q4 OKR is about. The elevation profile on the share page was the most commented
feature in the replies, which pleases me more than the sharing itself: it was the part I argued for in July, and the
telescope at Halden has taught me to respect the angle of elevation of anything.

## Notes from Jo
