# Kestrel 2.3

Released 2026-09-30. Route sharing, and the offline sync rewrite that makes editing work without a network. This
page is for customers and support; the engineering list is in the [changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md).

## Highlights

Share a route with anyone. Press **Share** on a saved route, copy the link and send it; the link works for three
days and you can stop it sooner. Details in [route sharing](../features/route-sharing.md).

Edit offline. Changes you make with no connection are kept on your device and sent when you are back online, in the
order you made them.

## Before you upgrade

- Browsers older than Safari 16 or Chrome 110 are no longer supported.
- Old `?route=` links keep working until 2026-12-31 and then stop.

## What to tell customers

Shared links expire after 72 hours. This is on purpose. If a customer needs a longer one, send them to the owner of
the route, who can share again.

## Known issues

- The "needs attention" list for parked sync ops is not in the app yet; support sees them in the admin tool.
- Elevation graphs on routes shorter than 500 m look flat.
