# How we review

Ask for review in the pull request, not in chat. A reviewer reads for the decision first and the prose second, and
approves when they could explain the change to a customer. Tomás reviews anything with a screenshot; Priya reviews
anything with a date.

## Before you ask

- Reread the page against [the style table](how-we-write.md#style): a number and a date, an owner for every question.
- Check that every link works from the file, and that dates are ISO ([the rules](how-we-write.md#rules-we-keep)).
- Use the words in [the glossary](glossary.md), and say in the pull request which decision you want a second pair of
  eyes on.

## Approving

A reviewer answers within two working days, as the [PRD process](../prd/plan.md) promises. "Approved" means they would
stand behind the page in a customer call; "approved with comments" means the comments are optional. Anything that
changes how we write goes through [the handbook page](/how-we-write.md) first, so the rules stay in one place.
