## O1: Ship route sharing (2.3)

Riders can send a route to anyone with a link, and the link works without the app. We ship it with **2.3** on 2026-09-30
and measure over the following 30 days.

- KR1.1 to KR1.3 above, as `shared per rider`, `non-member opens` and `sign-up rate`.
- Anything we learn that changes a goal goes in the PRD first, then here.
- ~~Show riders who opened their link~~ cut on 2026-09-17: privacy review wants a design first.

## O2: Offline that earns trust

The rider should never wonder whether a change was kept. The *queue* is durable, the *state* is visible, a stall is
detected by the app. KR2.2 is the one at risk: the incident of 2026-09-22 showed the drain is slow when a worker
restarts; after the fix it is 6.2 minutes against a target of 5.
