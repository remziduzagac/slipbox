## Rollout

| Stage | Share of riders | Hold for | Rollback if |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Dogfood | staff | 5 days | any lost change |
| Canary | 1% | 1 day | crash rate over 0.5% |
| Early | 10% | 2 days | stalled queues over 0.1% |
| Half | 25%, then 50% | two hours each | support tickets double |
| All | 100% | none | n/a |

## Risks

The sync incident of 2026-09-22 cost two days of the 10% stage, and the migration of the old queue is the part most
likely to hurt a rider, so the plan holds the 10% stage until the stalled-queue rate is below 0.1%.
