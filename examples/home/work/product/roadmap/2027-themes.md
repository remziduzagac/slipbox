# 2027 themes (draft)

A first list for the planning offsite in November. Nothing here is committed.

## Candidates

1. **Plan together.** Edit a route with friends: shared routes, comments, a vote on the stop for lunch. Builds on the
   share links of 2.3.
2. **Gravel and mixed surfaces.** A surface layer in the route engine, and routes that prefer or avoid it. Malik thinks
   the tile cost is fine.
3. **Event rides.** Brevets and club rides: a start list, a control schedule, a downloadable cue sheet.
4. **Better offline maps.** Tiles that update in the background, not only on demand.
5. **Accessibility pass.** Contrast, focus order and a screen-reader review of the map.

## What we would stop

- The web editor, unless usage rises: 4% of routes were edited on the web in Q3.
- The legacy GPX importer.

## Inputs still missing

- Two more interviews (Jo): someone who commutes, someone who tours.
- Cost of the surface layer (Aiko, Malik).
