Q4 2026 roadmap
===============

Three months, three themes, one person-week at a time. The objectives behind it are in [the OKRs](../okrs/2026-q4.md);
the requirement documents are linked from each theme. "W" is the ISO week: W41 starts on 2026-10-05.

Themes
------

1. **Route sharing** reaches everyone: 2.3 on 2026-09-30, iOS on 2026-10-03, then the numbers.
2. **Offline sync** gets its retry budget (R3) and stall detection (R6), then the follow-up work of the incident.
3. **Hiring** one backend engineer: the offer is out, and the start depends on an eight-week notice period.

A theme that slips pushes the one after it, not the one before. The release plan for the first is in
[the 2.3 plan](/releases/2.3-plan.md).
