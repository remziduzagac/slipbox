The rules for anyone working in the dome, in the order they matter. The long version is
xref:operations-handbook.md[the operations handbook]; the dome's repair history is
xref:../logs/dome-log.org[the dome log].

[[before]]
== Before you go up

WARNING: Never work alone in the dome after dark.

* [ ] tell someone in the control room
* [ ] check the wind: <<limits,the limits table>>
* [x] wear the head torch, red mode
** spare batteries are in the blue box

[[limits]]
== Wind and weather limits

.When to close
|===
| Condition | Limit | Action

| Wind | 40 km/h | close the shutter
| Humidity | 85 % | close, cap the optics
| Rain | any | close at once
|===

[TIP]
====
The weather station's page is at https://halden-ridge.invalid/weather[the weather page];
it updates every minute.
====

== After

. Park the telescope.
. Close the shutter and check it latched.
. Log the session: see <<before>> for what to check next time.

[source,bash]
----
halley dome close --verify
----
